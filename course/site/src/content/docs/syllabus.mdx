description: Eleven weeks, four days a week, covering about 80% of The Mechanics of Proof.

Each week has three **lesson days** and one **lab day**. On the lab day you work on that week's homework set. In weeks 5 and 10 you also do a **checkpoint**: unseen Lean problems where you explain your reasoning to your teacher. A lesson takes about 45–60 minutes and always ends at a stopping point.

Numbers like **1.1** are sections of the book.
