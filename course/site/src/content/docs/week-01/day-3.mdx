## 1.4 Proving inequalities

<Book file={CH1} section="proving-inequalities" parts={["id33"]} />

<CourseNote kind="algebra" title="Algebra refresher: which way does an inequality go?">
Three rules cover almost everything in this section:

- **Adding or subtracting** the same number from both sides keeps the direction: $a \le b$ gives $a + 5 \le b + 5$.
- **Multiplying by a positive** number keeps the direction: $a \le b$ gives $3a \le 3b$.
- **Multiplying by a negative** number **flips** it: $a \le b$ gives $-2a \ge -2b$.

That last rule is why the step "$9 - 2(x+3) \ge 9 - 2 \cdot 2$" uses $\ge$, even though the fact is $x + 3 \le 2$. Drag $x$ below and watch the two dots move in opposite directions:
